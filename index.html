Driveline
Calculator Suite

For years I did what most engineers do, kept a folder of scattered Excel sheets, jumped between browser tabs, & rebuilt the same calculations from scratch every time a new project started. It worked, but it wasn't exactly efficient.

So I built something similar instead. Every calculator here started as something I genuinely needed at my desk, a quicker way to check a number, validate a design decision, or explain a result to a customer without hunting through a spreadsheet at 9am.

These are standard mechanical engineering calculations, but the point isn't the maths but it's the approach. If I can build this for my own domain, I can build the same thing for yours. Whether your team runs on MS Power Apps, SharePoint, or a simple internal web tool, the goal is the same: less time on repetitive calculations, more time on actual engineering.
