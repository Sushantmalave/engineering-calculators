// 03 · Formula Reference
ID = OD − (2 × Wall Thickness)
Area = π/4 × (OD² − ID²)
Volume = Area × Length
Weight (g) = Volume (cm³) × Density (g/cm³)
Weight (kg) = Weight (g) / 1000

Standard material densities used per ASM Handbook.
