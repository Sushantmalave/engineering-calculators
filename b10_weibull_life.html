Formula References & Standards ▼
Weibull Distribution
F(t) = 1 − e−(t/η)β
Source: Weibull, W. (1951). "A Statistical Distribution Function of Wide Applicability." Journal of Applied Mechanics, 18(3), pp. 293–297. ASME. Open-access seminal paper — public domain.
Median Ranks — Benard's Approximation
Fi = (i − 0.3) / (n + 0.4)
Source: Benard, A. & Bos-Levenbach, E.C. (1953). "The Plotting of Observations on Probability Paper." Statistica Neerlandica, 7(3), pp. 163–173. Standard approximation universally adopted in reliability engineering.
Median Ranks — Hazen & Mean Rank
Hazen: Fi = (i − 0.5) / n
Mean: Fi = i / (n + 1)
Source: Nelson, W. (1982). Applied Life Data Analysis. John Wiley & Sons, pp. 141–143. Standard reference for plotting position formulas.
Rank Regression — RRY & RRX
Linearisation: Y = β·X + C where X = ln(t), Y = ln(−ln(1−F))
RRY: OLS minimising vertical residuals. RRX: OLS minimising horizontal residuals (default in ReliaSoft Weibull++).
Source: Meeker, W.Q. & Escobar, L.A. (1998). Statistical Methods for Reliability Data. John Wiley & Sons, Ch. 6.
Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)
Log-likelihood: ℓ(β,η) = r·ln(β/ηβ) + (β−1)·Σln(ti) − Σ(tj/η)β
Solved via Newton-Raphson iteration on the β score equation.
Source: Lawless, J.F. (2003). Statistical Models and Methods for Lifetime Data. 2nd ed. John Wiley & Sons, Ch. 4.
B-Life (B10) Calculation
Bp = η · (−ln(1 − p/100))1/β
For B10: p = 0.10, giving B10 = η · (−ln 0.9)1/β
Source: O'Connor, P.D.T. & Kleyner, A. (2012). Practical Reliability Engineering. 5th ed. John Wiley & Sons, Ch. 2–3.
Automotive Application Standard
B10 / L10 life concept and two-parameter Weibull application to component testing:
ISO 281:2007 — Rolling bearings: Dynamic load ratings and rating life.
SAE J1099 — Technical Report on Fatigue Properties.
Individual minimum + B10 dual-requirement structure per OEM DVP&R practice (e.g. GM BIQS, Ford Q1).
